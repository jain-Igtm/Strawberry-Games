# The Lantern Road

A playable Godot 4 side-scrolling fantasy adventure prototype. Open **project.godot** in Godot 4.3+ and press Play.

**Controls:** A/D or arrows move, Space jumps, J attacks, E talks/interacts, Escape closes dialogue. On Android, use the on-screen touch buttons. Find the bell keeper, explore the forest, and descend into the ruins. Progress saves locally after major events.

This is a self-contained original game prototype with procedural pixel art, three connected regions, branching dialogue, enemy AI, collectible lantern fragments, combat, and a boss. No external art or paid assets are needed. Export an Android APK using Godot's Android export templates and an installed Android SDK.
