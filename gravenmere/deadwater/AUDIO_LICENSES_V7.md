# Ashfall: Deadwater — recorded audio sources

The v7 zombie voices and sirens are packaged into the APK for offline playback.

All files listed here are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal).

## Zombie voices

- `zombie-groan-1.ogg`
  - Original: **Zombie moans** by Darsycho
  - Source: OpenGameArt, `https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-moans`
  - License: CC0
- `zombie-groan-2.ogg` and `zombie-groan-3.ogg`
  - Original: **Zombie Moans 01** by EmoPreben
  - Source: OpenGameArt, `https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-moans-01-by-emopreben`
  - License: CC0
  - OGG conversions are mirrored by `prestonpope192/zombie_invasion`, whose audio credits document the original files and conversion.
