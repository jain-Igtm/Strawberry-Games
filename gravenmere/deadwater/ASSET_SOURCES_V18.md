# Ashfall v0.18 asset sources

## Dissipating nuclear plume

- Source: *Atomic cloud over Hiroshima – NARA 542192 – Edit*
- Photograph: George R. Caron, U.S. Army Air Forces, 6 August 1945
- Archive: U.S. National Archives and Records Administration, NAID 542192
- License: Public domain in the United States (U.S. federal government work)
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atomic_cloud_over_Hiroshima_-_NARA_542192_-_Edit.jpg
- Game treatment: cropped, downsampled, desaturated, and rendered as a static
  charcoal post-blast plume with soft alpha falloff.

## Animated zombie

- Source: *Zombie* by Pixelhouse
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
- Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/zombie
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/zombie.zip
- Game treatment: the authored low-poly mesh, UVs, rig, diffuse texture, walk,
  fury/attack, and death animations are preserved. FBX animation files were
  converted to glTF, the 2048px diffuse map was reduced to a 512px WebP, and
  expensive normal/specular maps were omitted for mobile performance.

Credit: Pixelhouse — https://opengameart.org/users/pixelhouse
